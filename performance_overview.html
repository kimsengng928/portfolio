Performance Overview provides a unified approach to employee engagement and professional development

 

Project Summary

The objective behind implementing this feature is to increase employee engagement by providing more professional development opportunities.

The key problem to solve is creating a strong talent experience for employees and managers. This requires creating a centralised space for individuals to view their talent activity without visiting multiple areas.

My Role

Main areas of responsibility:

  • User interview
  • Competitive product research
  • Concept ideation
  • Designed user flows
  • Prototyping
  • Usability testing
  • Created detailed design spec for implementation.
  • Ensure the design met the accessibility standards.

The Challenge

Collecting feedback from end users via our partners was challenging. Despite our efforts to distribute surveys and follow up with partners, we faced considerable obstacles in obtaining their input, with time growing increasingly limited.

The Solutions

We hypothesised that providing an overview of an individual’s talent achievements can substantially improve the value of the ‘meaningful conversations’ required for effective talent management.

We validated the hypothesis by looking at our partners’ and competitors’ solutions, and we can see many examples of a talent summary in both partner custom implementations and competitor products.

Our user testing with internal people discovered that this summary effectively led the employee to their talent progress discovery. The first step to creating a meaningful conversation.

The Result

The feature has been released in T18 and has received positive feedback from our partners. Users expressed that having a summary of their performance items significantly eased their preparation for performance reviews. Additionally, managers found it easier to identify if any support was needed for their team members.
