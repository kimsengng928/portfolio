MBIE Earthquake Repairs- Jacking Up A Foundation

 

Project Summary

The MBIE Earthquake repairs project provides online resources to clarify key sections of the Residential Building Repair and Reconstruction Guidance. I collaborated with builders in Canterbury to create step-by-step videos demonstrating repair techniques. This video focuses on safely and properly jacking up foundations to re-level floors in earthquake-damaged houses.
